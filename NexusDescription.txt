[color=#ffff00][size=4]Features[/size][/color]
[color=#ffd966]Expansive Practice - longer and more intense fights, better rewards and an optional way to train your party troops.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Team Practice - even more intense fights where you fight together with your clan heroes as a team.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Parry Practice - helps you practice parrying and chamber blocking.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Fight as a companion in any Arena Practice mode.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Weapon preferences for companions and other clan heroes in Arena Practice, so they train the combat skills you want them to train.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Many adjustable arena practice settings: match length and intensity, number of AI teams, type of defensive equipment.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Choose your weapon loadout for arena practice fights (idea and some parts of implementation taken from mewhi's Arena Expansion mod).[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]List of ongoing tournaments in the arena menu (idea and some parts of implementation taken from Operation40's Arena Helper mod).[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Many adjustable tournament reward settings. The defaults keep tournaments rewarding through the whole game while staying balanced.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]High quality items (fine, masterwork, lordly, etc) as tournament prizes.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Everyone receives all the tournament rewards, player or not. In vanilla, AI got no renown or influence rewards.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Team tournaments (idea and implementation by eg3r - thank you for the contribution!).[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Adjustable XP gain for arena practice and tournaments.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Tournament injuries - every hero knocked out of a tournament match (player, companions and lords alike) loses hit points and carries that injury back onto the campaign map.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Tournament hosting - host your own tournament in a town that currently has none. Costs gold and influence scaled by town prosperity and your clan standing, with adjustable cooldown and affiliation requirements.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Yearly Grand Tournament of Calradia and seasonal Kingdom Tournaments - 1v1 bracket events held in the capital, where the strongest lords of the arena leaderboard are summoned from all over the map. Legendary quality prizes, big gold, renown and influence rewards.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Map popup notifications for Grand and Kingdom Tournament announcements and results. Click the popup for details and to jump to the host town.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Works with or without Mod Configuration Menu.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Translations for 12 languages.[/color]

[color=#6fa8dc]Without Mod Configuration Menu:[/color] open [color=#ffd966]Modules[/color]\[color=#f1c232]ArenaOverhaulRedux[/color]\[color=#ffd966]ModConfiguration.xml[/color] in any text editor. [color=#ffff00][size=4]Compatibility[/size][/color]
Can be added [color=#93c47d]anytime[/color]. Grand and Kingdom Tournaments [color=#e06666]add data[/color] to save game files, so if you want to remove the mod, wait until there is no ongoing Grand or Kingdom Tournament and dismiss their map popups first.
Uses Harmony patches on the arena practice and tournament code. Mods that change the same parts of the game may conflict.
[color=#e06666]Important[/color]: If using together with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/791]Realistic Battle Mod[/url] then [color=#e06666]disable[/color] [color=#ffd966]RBM Tournament Module[/color] in RBM Configuration settings.
Works with [color=#ffd966]Tournaments XPanded[/color] - its prize re-roll and prize selection options are hidden for Grand and Kingdom Tournaments, since their prizes are fixed.

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Installation[/size][/color]
Updating? [color=#e06666]Delete the old mod folder first[/color], never overwrite it.
Advisable to use [color=#ffd966]7zip[/color] to extract archive.
Put it in [color=#ffd966]Mount & Blade II Bannerlord[/color]\[color=#f1c232]Modules[/color] folder.
Enable the mod in the launcher. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2006]Harmony[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018]ButterLib[/url], [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2102]UIExtenderEx[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/612]Mod Configuration Menu[/url] (if you use it) must be above it.

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Found a problem?[/size][/color]
Generate a crash report link using [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018]ButterLib[/url] mod. How to do that the mini guide is here: [url=http://bannerlordmodding.lt/guides/how_to_report_a_crash/]How To Report A Crash[/url]
Turn on [color=#ffd966]Logging for debugging[/color] in [color=#ffd966]MCM[/color] settings or [color=#ffd966]ModConfiguration.xml[/color] in mod folder, [color=#f6b26b]repeat the problem[/color], then attach the file from [color=#ffd966]Modules[/color]\[color=#f1c232]ArenaOverhaulRedux[/color]\[color=#ffd966]logs[/color] to your [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018]ButterLib[/url] crash report.
